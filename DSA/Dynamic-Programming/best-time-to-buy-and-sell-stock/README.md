## 📝 Problem Statement

Given an array arr of n integers, where arr[i] represents price of the stock on the ith day.&nbsp;Determine the **maximum profit** achievable by buying and selling the stock at most once.&nbsp;

The stock should be purchased before selling it, and both actions cannot occur on the same day.

### Example 1

<p>

**Input:** arr = [10, 7, 5, 8, 11, 9]</p><p>

**Output:** 6</p><p>

**Explanation:** Buy on day 3 (price = 5) and sell on day 5 (price = 11), profit = 11 - 5 = 6.</p>

### Example 2

<p>

**Input:** &nbsp;arr = [5, 4, 3, 2, 1]</p><p>

**Output:** 0</p><p>

**Explanation:** In this case, no transactions are made. Therefore, the maximum profit remains 0.</p>

### Constraints

- 1 <= n<= 10^5
- 0 <= arr[i] <= 10^6

---

## 💡 Complexity Analysis

- **Time Complexity:** $\mathcal{O}(N)$
- **Space Complexity:** $\mathcal{O}(1)$
